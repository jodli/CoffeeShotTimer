THESIS: Onboarding is the bench demonstration. It shows the brewing control chart and the equipment ledger before the user commits: set grinder scale and basket weights on ruled forms, review them as figures, create the first bag, pull the first shot.
OWN-WORLD: Paper ground, one ink. Zone colours carry meaning only (gold resting, green fresh/dialed-in, ink past peak; sour gold and bitter rust on the chart). Archivo figures, condensed caps labels, tabular numerals. Square corners, hairline rules. The shot chart's dotted trajectory is the only ornament. No cards, shadows, pills, circles-as-decor or emoji.
STORY: A new user skips or swipes five short slides — the welcome slide shows a real dial-in plotted from sour to on-target — sets up equipment in four ruled steps with a square step track, meets the bean form they will use every week, and lands on Record Shot.
