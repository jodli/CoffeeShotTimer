THESIS: Beans is the shelf of bags beside the grinder. One ruled line per bag tells you which is in use, how far past roast it is, and where its dial-in stands; one tap puts it in the grinder. Refuses the card stack, chip rows and per-card buttons.
OWN-WORLD: Paper ground, one ink. Zone colours carry meaning only: gold = still resting, green = fresh / dialed in, ink = past peak. Quality status sits on the single green scale, never red. Archivo figures, condensed caps labels, tabular numerals. Square corners, hairline rules, an ink bar marks the bag in use. No cards, shadows, pills or emoji.
STORY: Open Beans, see the bag in use marked and every other bag's roast age and status at a glance, tap one to brew with it, pencil to edit. The edit form reads as ruled sections (details, photo, status), with roast age judged live as you set the date.
